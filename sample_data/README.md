# Synthetic sample corpus

Every file in this directory is fictional and exists only to exercise the RAG pipeline. Agency names, people, program names, dates, dollar amounts, solicitation numbers, and awards are invented. They must never be presented as government facts. Documents dated after the real current date are **future-dated fictional scenario evidence**, not historical records that were genuinely published or retrieved.

The corpus forms a small evidence chain for the fictional **Northstar Department of Resident Services (NDRS)**:

1. `01_agency_strategy.txt` states a citizen-service modernization need.
2. `02_budget_evidence.txt` supplies related budget evidence.
3. `03_modernization_rfp.txt` solicits the work.
4. `04_contract_award.txt` records only a fictional **notice of intent to award**. It is not evidence of a signed contract.
5. `05_program_integrity.txt` uses varied terminology for payment accuracy and fraud/program integrity.
6. `06_confirmed_contract_award.txt` separately records a fictional executed contract with a contract ID, selected vendor, execution date, and finalized scope.
