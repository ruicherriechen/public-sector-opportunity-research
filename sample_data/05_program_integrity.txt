Northstar Department of Resident Services
Benefits Stewardship and Payment Quality Plan
Publication date: 2028-05-10
Last updated: 2028-06-01

1. Payment Quality

NDRS will improve benefit correctness by identifying both overpayments and underpayments before issuance when practical. The department uses the phrase payment accuracy to include the correct recipient, amount, and period of eligibility. Quality-control samples will distinguish agency processing error, incomplete information, and timing differences rather than labeling every discrepancy as intentional misconduct.

2. Program Integrity

The stewardship program will combine preventive controls, post-payment review, and targeted case examination. Cross-program data matching may flag inconsistent identity, income, residency, or household information for review. A match is an investigative lead, not a final determination, and trained staff must document resolution.

3. Fraud Risk Management

Potential fraud means deliberate deception for an unauthorized benefit. Detection analytics will prioritize unusual patterns, coordinated account activity, and repeated document attributes. The agency will monitor false positives, provide notice and appeal rights, and prohibit automatic adverse action based solely on a model score.

4. Loss Prevention and Recovery

Improper payment reduction includes process correction, recipient education, verification at application or renewal, and recovery when legally authorized. The plan also calls this work benefits assurance and leakage control. These related terms may appear in different program materials even when the underlying modernization need is shared.

5. Technology Needs

NDRS needs configurable rules, entity resolution, case-review workflow, evidence tracking, audit history, and outcome measurement. Any future acquisition must separate detection from adjudication and provide transparent reasons that reviewers can evaluate.
